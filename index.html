Why Hokkaido

Japan's Food Paradise

Hokkaido produces over 20% of Japan's agricultural output from just 20% of the land. The result: ingredients and products of unparalleled quality.

🥛
World-Class Dairy
Hokkaido's cool climate and vast pastures produce Japan's richest butter, cream and milk — the foundation of iconic products like Royce Nama Chocolate and Rokkatei's buttery classics.
🥔
Premium Potatoes
The volcanic soil of Hokkaido yields Japan's finest potatoes. Calbee's Hokkaido-exclusive snacks are made from crops harvested just kilometres from their factory — unbeatable freshness.
🏔
Artisan Heritage
Hokkaido's confectionery culture stretches back over a century. Brands like Rokkatei and Ishiya have spent generations refining their craft — producing sweets that are truly irreplaceable.
